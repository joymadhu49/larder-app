Larder

Effective 29 September 2026

Privacy policy

Short version: Larder does not collect any data about you. Your lists, pantry, prices and trips are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What Larder stores

The items, stock levels, prices, notes and shopping trips you enter are saved in the app's private storage on your device, along with your settings (such as currency and units). This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Larder does not connect to any server.

If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, this data is included in that device backup like other app data, under Apple's terms.

Camera and photos

When you choose to scan a receipt, Larder uses the camera, or a photo you pick, only to read the text on that receipt. Text recognition runs on your device. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Larder uses Apple's on-device model to tidy item names; this also happens on your device. Receipt images are not saved by Larder and are never uploaded. Larder can only see photos you pick yourself.

Backups and imports

If you export a backup, Larder creates a file and hands it to the place you choose, such as Files, iCloud Drive or email. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you and the service you pick. Imported lists and backups are read on your device only.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers.
  • We do not use third party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

Deleting your data

You can remove everything at any time in Settings, Erase everything. Deleting the app also deletes all of its data from your device.

Children

Larder does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy are welcome. See the support page for how to reach us.
