Common questions

How does an item get on my list by itself?

When you mark a pantry item as Running low or Out, Larder adds it to your list. You can turn this off in Settings, Add low items to list.

What happens when I finish shopping?

Items you ticked are marked Full in your pantry, taken off the list, and the trip is saved with its total. You can undo right after.

How does receipt scanning work?

Tap the scan button at the top of your List (or Settings, Scan a receipt), then take a photo or pick one. Larder reads the receipt on your phone, matches lines to your items and lets you review before saving prices. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings, Export backup, and save the file to iCloud Drive or send it to yourself. On the new phone, open Settings, Restore backup, and pick that file.

Can I change the currency?

Yes, in Settings, Currency. Saved prices are kept as plain numbers; Larder asks whether to keep or clear them.

How do I delete my data?

Settings, Erase everything removes your pantry, list, prices and trips. Deleting the app removes everything too.
